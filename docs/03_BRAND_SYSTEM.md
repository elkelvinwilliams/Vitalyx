## 1. Positioning

**Category:** A UK health-technology group that combines advisory, consulting, product development and learning under one brand.

**Positioning statement (internal):**
For health organisations, founders and public bodies that need technology to work in real care settings, Vytalix is a health-technology group that brings strategic advice, hands-on delivery, product development and learning together — because the gap between a good idea and a safe, adopted product is closed by people who can do all four.

**What makes it distinct (true today):**
- Four connected pillars rather than a single consultancy or a single product. Advice informs delivery; delivery informs product; product informs learning.
- Founder-led, pre-formation, honest about stage. Our credibility comes from rigour and candour, not from a claimed track record.
- An explicit safety posture: MaternaLink v1 is positioned as non-diagnostic care-coordination and monitoring-support, with clinical AI held as a research track until validated (FACTS_BASE A4).
- A dual focus: UK home market, with active interest in maternal health in Nigeria and one further African market (FACTS_BASE A2).

**Positioning we must NOT adopt:** "the AI that predicts maternal complications", "NHS partner", "award-winning", "trusted by".

## 2. Brand promise

**Technology that earns its place in care.**
Everything we advise on, build or teach must be safe, adoptable and worth the attention of the people who use it.

## 3. Brand personality

| Trait | What it looks like | What it never looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Intelligent | Precise language, evidence cited, trade-offs named | Jargon, buzzwords, unsupported superlatives |
| Human | Writes to a person; acknowledges the clinician, the mother, the manager | Stock photos of smiling doctors; "users" as an abstraction |
| Technological | Confident about systems, data, standards, engineering | Circuit-board clichés, glowing brains, "AI-powered everything" |
| Trustworthy | Says what it is and isn't; marks drafts as drafts | Overclaiming; vague "partners" |
| Premium | Restraint, whitespace, typographic care | Cheap templates, clip art, gradient sludge |
| Innovative | New combinations of proven things; thoughtful roadmap | Novelty for its own sake |
| Impact-driven | Names the problem (e.g. maternal inequity) plainly, commits to measuring | Charity-style pity imagery; virtue signalling |

## 4. Tone of voice

**Principles:** clear before clever; specific before broad; honest about stage; warm but not chatty; British English.

**Do / Don't**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "MaternaLink is in development. It is designed to support care coordination; it is not a diagnostic tool." | "MaternaLink predicts and prevents maternal complications." |
| "We are founder-led and pre-seed. Here is exactly what we can do for you today." | "Trusted by leading NHS trusts." |
| "We'll tell you if your idea should not be built." | "We turn every vision into reality." |
| "Accreditation is being explored; our courses are not yet accredited." | "CPD-certified courses." |
| "Register interest — we'll publish roles when we can fund them." | "Join our fast-growing team!" |
| "Built with clinicians" — **only once a clinician has actually co-designed it.** | Claiming co-design before it happens. |

**Micro-rules**
- Sentences under 25 words where possible. One idea per paragraph.
- Use "we" and "you". Avoid "the company" and passive constructions.
- Numbers: cite the source or don't use the number. The MBRRACE-UK disparity statistic may be used only with the current report cited (FACTS_BASE §1).
- Never write "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "world-class", "seamless", "leverage" (verb), "solutions" as a filler noun (Health Solutions is a pillar name and is fine).
- Mark placeholders in square brackets: `[Registered office address — add after incorporation]`.

## 8. Typography

**Pairing (Google Fonts):** **Fraunces** (display/headings — a warm, contemporary serif with optical sizes; reads premium and human, avoids the SaaS-sans sameness) + **Manrope** (body/UI — geometric, highly legible, technical without coldness).

```css
--font-display: "Fraunces", "Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Georgia, serif;
--font-body: "Manrope", "Inter", system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif;
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, monospace; /* not loaded; system fallback */
```

**Scale (web, fluid):** base 17px/1.6; h1 clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 4rem) weight 500, letter-spacing −0.02em, line-height 1.05; h2 clamp(1.8rem, 3.2vw, 2.6rem); h3 1.35rem (Manrope 700); eyebrow 0.8rem uppercase tracking 0.12em (Manrope 700, teal).
Fraunces is loaded with the `SOFT` axis at a low value and `opsz` auto so it stays crisp, not decorative. Weights loaded: Fraunces 400/500/600; Manrope 400/500/700. Load with `font-display: swap` and `preconnect`.

**Print/deck equivalents when fonts cannot be embedded:** Georgia + Arial.

## 9. Logo

**Wordmark concept — "the monoline mark":** VYTALIX set in a custom geometric monoline (every letter built from straight strokes, round caps, uniform weight). The letters V, Y, T, A, L, I, X are all straight-line forms, which makes a bespoke, fully-outlined wordmark possible without any font dependency — it renders identically everywhere and can be cut, embroidered or engraved.

**Symbol concept — "the Y-node":** the Y in VYTALIX is the symbol. Its three strokes stand for the three things Vytalix brings together — people, care and data (or advice, delivery and product). Where the strokes meet, a small filled circle in Ember marks the point of convergence: the moment technology becomes useful in a life. The Y-node is extracted as the standalone mark for favicons, app icons and social avatars, placed inside a rounded square in Deep with the strokes in Bone and the node in Ember.

**Files:** `website/assets/img/logo.svg` (primary wordmark, positive), `logo-mono.svg` (single colour), `logo-reverse.svg` (for dark surfaces), `mark.svg` (Y-node symbol), `favicon.svg`.

**Rules:** minimum width 120px / 30mm for the wordmark, 24px for the mark; clear space = height of the letter X on every side; never stretch, recolour outside the palette, add drop shadows or place on photography without a solid panel. Do not typeset the name in Fraunces or Manrope as a substitute for the wordmark; in running text write "Vytalix" (title case).

## 11. Illustration and photography direction

**Illustration (primary visual layer):** abstract "confluence" compositions — flowing monoline paths converging to nodes on Deep/Night panels with Signal Mint and Ember accents; soft topographic contour fields on Bone. They express coordination, signal and care without depicting bodies, organs or devices. All current site illustrations are SVG and stock-free.

**Photography (when budget allows; none used today):**
- Real environments, real light, real people at work — corridors, community clinics, kitchen-table meetings, a midwife's bag, a laptop on a ward desk. Documentary, not posed.
- Diverse by default, particularly in maternal contexts, and always with consent and model releases.
- No white-coat-and-stethoscope stock, no glowing-brain AI, no hands-on-belly clichés.
- Colour grade: warm neutrals, slight desaturation; never blue-tinted.
- Until real photography is commissioned, use illustration. Never use stock photography that implies a clinical partnership we do not have.

## 12. UI design principles

1. **Say what it is.** Every product surface states its stage and scope (e.g. "In development — non-diagnostic").
2. **One accent, used for meaning.** Ember marks the action or the node, nothing else.
3. **Generous rhythm.** 8px spacing scale; section padding 96–128px on desktop; max line length 65–72ch.
4. **Accessible by construction.** Semantic landmarks, skip link, visible focus (2px Ember ring with 2px offset), 4.5:1 contrast, reduced-motion respected, 44px touch targets.
5. **Fast by default.** No frameworks, no third-party scripts except Google Fonts; SVG only; total page weight under 300KB before fonts.
6. **Cards are for choices, not decoration.** Cards only where the user picks between siblings (pillars, packages).
7. **Dark theme is a treatment, not a second brand.** Same tokens, inverted surfaces; Ember Light and Signal Mint carry accent duties.
8. **Forms are honest.** Tell people where the data goes and what happens next.

## 13. Presentation design system

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080. Margins 96px. 12-column grid with 24px gutters; content area 1728px.
- **Title slide:** Deep background, wordmark reverse top-left, Fraunces 500 title 72px in Bone, subtitle Manrope 28px in Signal Mint, footer line with date and "Confidential — draft" in Night Muted.
- **Section divider:** Night background, confluence illustration right two-thirds, section number in Ember 24px, title 56px.
- **Body slide:** Bone background; eyebrow (Manrope 700, 18px, uppercase, Teal); title Fraunces 44px Ink; body Manrope 24px/1.4 Slate, max 2 columns; footer: slide number left, "Vytalix — Technology for Life" centre, status tag right.
- **Data slide:** one chart per slide; Teal primary series, Slate secondary, Ember for the one point that matters; source line in 16px under the chart.
- **Rules:** no more than 6 bullets; no clip art; every number has a source or a label of ESTIMATE/ASSUMPTION; roadmap slides show validation gates explicitly.
- Fonts embedded; fallback Georgia/Arial.

**Proposal template structure:**
1. Cover — client name, engagement title, date, version, "Proposal — confidential".
2. Summary on one page — the problem, what we propose, outcome, price, timing.

5. Deliverables (table: deliverable, format, acceptance criterion).
6. Team and roles (founder-led; named associates only with consent; no invented people).
7. Timeline (Gantt-style table).
8. Commercials (fixed fee or day rate; payment schedule; expenses; VAT status TO VALIDATE).
9. Assumptions, dependencies and risks.
10. Terms (reference to Terms of Business; IP; confidentiality; data protection).
11. Next steps and signature block.
Appendix: about Vytalix (stage-honest one-pager); relevant methods.

## 15. Social-media design (LinkedIn)

**Templates (1080×1350 portrait for feed, 1200×627 for link posts):**
- **Insight post:** Bone background, eyebrow "Vytalix Insight" in Teal, headline Fraunces 64px Ink (max 12 words), small Y-node bottom-right, page footer with the wordmark.
- **Quote/Stat post:** Deep background, stat or line in Fraunces 96px Bone with the key figure in Signal Mint, source line 24px Night Muted at the bottom. No stat without a source.
- **Announcement post:** Night background with confluence illustration, eyebrow "Update", headline 56px, one-line detail, CTA "Read more — link in comments".

**Carousel structure (PDF, 1080×1350, 6–10 slides):**
1. Hook — one claim we can stand behind, or one question.
2. Why it matters (with source).
3–7. One idea per slide, ≤ 30 words, consistent eyebrow numbering "02 / 08".
8. What we'd do about it (the practical move).
9. Honesty slide — "What we don't know yet" (this becomes a signature).
10. Close — wordmark, "Technology for Life", how to get in touch.

**Voice on LinkedIn:** founder's first person is fine; company page uses "we". No engagement-bait, no "🚀". Stage disclosure in the About section: "Pre-seed, founder-led. MaternaLink is in development."

# 12 — VYTALIX PARTNERSHIP STRATEGY

**Owner:** Chief Strategy Officer · **Status:** PROPOSED v1 · **Date:** 8 September 2026 · **Governing file:** `/FACTS_BASE.md` (KNOWN / VERIFIED / ASSUMPTION / ESTIMATE / PROPOSED / TO VALIDATE apply throughout). **Vytalix has no partners today** — no MoU, LOI, contract, grant, membership or introduction with any organisation named in this document (VERIFIED absence, FACTS_BASE §1). Every organisation below is named as a public institution and a target; nothing here may be read, quoted or presented as a relationship. Every score is a desk ESTIMATE to be re-scored after first contact.

## Executive view

1. **Partnerships are the route to market for everything Vytalix cannot buy or build alone: NHS access, clinical credibility, an African cohort, evidence, and non-dilutive money.** A solo, pre-formation founder has no references; partners substitute for them. The plan therefore treats partnerships as the second commercial engine beside founder-led services, not as a corporate-development afterthought.
2. **Twenty-five organisations were scored on seven weighted criteria (§3).** The top tier for the next 12 months is a regional Health Innovation Network (86/100), Five X More (76), Africa's Talking (76), Society for Family Health Nigeria (74) and the DigitalHealth.London Accelerator (73) — organisations that are reachable by a solo founder, low-cost to engage and directly unlock either NHS introductions, community co-design, a Nigerian cohort or the messaging rail MaternaLink (in development) needs.
3. **Three relationships are gated, not scored down: mDoc, MSD for Mothers and any government.** mDoc is the funded incumbent in Ekiti (VERIFIED); MSD for Mothers funds it. Both are approached only with complementarity language, never with the March 2026 Ekiti proposal, and only after the MaternaLink IP question is settled (FACTS_BASE §1). Government relationships are pursued through implementing partners and donors, and an MoU is never described as a "government partnership".
4. **Six partnership models are used (§4) and each has a default contract form and an economic rule:** referral (fee-transparent, Bribery Act-safe), co-development (paid community partners, joint IP terms), distribution (revenue share 10–30%, ESTIMATE), data (controller/processor clarity, no individual data to insurers), research (co-applicant, shared authorship, ethics first), funding (grants where a UK entity and a clinical partner exist). Charities and community organisations are always paid for co-design; unpaid "endorsement" is never asked for.
5. **The 12-month plan (§5) targets, by month 12: one Health Innovation Network programme place, two paid community co-design agreements, one university co-applicant agreement, one Nigerian implementing-partner LOI for a cohort, one messaging-gateway pilot account, one NHS design-partner conversation opened through a clinical adviser, and two joint grant bids submitted** (PROPOSED). None of these is revenue in year 1; all of them are what an investor, a trust or a ministry will ask to see in year 2.

## 0. Method, definitions and rules of engagement

| Term | Meaning in this document |
|---|---|
| Partner | An organisation with which Vytalix has a written arrangement (LOI, MoU, agreement, programme place, grant award, sub-contract). Until a document exists, an organisation is a **target**, and a conversation is a **conversation**. |
| Ecosystem ring | Ring 1 = controls access to buyers or users; Ring 2 = confers credibility or evidence; Ring 3 = supplies money, rails or reach. Many organisations sit in two rings. |
| Model | One of six structures in §4: referral, co-development, distribution, data, research, funding (plus sub-contracting and content licensing as variants). |
| Score | Seven weighted criteria in §2; 0–100; ESTIMATE. |
| Horizon | **Now** (first contact within 90 days of incorporation); **6m**; **12m**; **24m+**. |

**Rules of engagement (apply to every outreach, deck, website page and investor conversation):**

1. Never name an organisation as a partner, supporter, client, endorser or "in discussion" without a signed document or their written permission for the wording (FACTS_BASE §4).
2. Never restate the March 2026 Ekiti proposal (£4.5m / £300 per woman per year) to any organisation; it is UNVALIDATED and it is not Vytalix's document (FACTS_BASE §1).
3. Never approach a donor, ministry or NGO about MaternaLink (in development) until the IP relationship with David Agunede is documented (FACTS_BASE §1). Services and E-Learning partnerships are not gated.
4. Pay community organisations and charities for co-design, content and listening exercises; do not ask for endorsement, logos or unpaid advocacy.
5. Referral fees, commissions and revenue shares are written down, disclosed to the end customer where NHS conflict-of-interest guidance or the Bribery Act 2010 requires it, and never paid to public officials or their relatives. A partnership register (see §5.6) records every arrangement.
6. Contact details are taken only from public sources; where none exist, the CRM records NOT PUBLICLY AVAILABLE.
7. Every conversation is logged in the CRM within 24 hours with the next dated action (`/docs/13_SALES_ENGINE.md` discipline applies to partners as to customers).

## 2. Partnership scoring framework (PROPOSED)

Seven criteria; weights sum to 100; each criterion scored 1–5 (5 = most favourable); **score = Σ(weight × criterion score) ÷ 5**, giving 0–100. Cost and risk are scored inversely (5 = low cost, low risk).

| # | Criterion | Weight | What "5" means | What "1" means |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strategic value | 20 | Unlocks a gate in the plan (NHS design partner, African cohort, evidence, IP-safe content) that nothing else unlocks | Nice to have; no gate opened |
| 2 | Revenue potential (24 months) | 15 | Direct fees, sub-contract days or programme licence ≥ £50k (ESTIMATE) | £0 and no plausible path |
| 3 | Credibility conferred | 15 | Named association would change a trust's, donor's or investor's view immediately | Unknown to buyers and investors |
| 4 | Access (can the founder reach a decision-maker within 30 days?) | 15 | Public application route or founder-level contact; answers in weeks | Requires an introduction Vytalix does not have; institutional gatekeeping |
| 5 | Time-to-value | 15 | Signed document or programme place within 3 months | 12+ months to any document |
| 6 | Cost to engage (inverse) | 10 | Under 3 founder days and < £1k to reach a decision | > 20 founder days, match funding, travel or legal cost > £10k |
| 7 | Risk (inverse) | 10 | No IP, reputational, dependency or conflict risk | Dependency on a competitor's goodwill, IP exposure, political or payment risk |

**Gates applied before scoring is used (a "no" on any gate pauses outreach regardless of score):**

| Gate | Applies to | Condition to pass |
|---|---|---|
| G1 — Entity | Any signed document; any grant | Vytalix incorporated, bank account, professional indemnity in place |
| G2 — IP | Any conversation about MaternaLink (in development) | Written agreement with David Agunede on MaternaLink IP (FACTS_BASE §1) |
| G3 — Claims | Any external material | Intended-use statement adopted; 2025 deck retired externally (FACTS_BASE A4) |
| G4 — Conflict | Any competitor or incumbent | Conflict register entry; partner-first wording approved (§5.5) |
| G5 — Payment integrity | Any government, official or referral arrangement | Bribery Act 2010 check; written fee terms; no payments to officials |

Services and E-Learning partnerships pass G1 and G3 only. Scores are re-run after every first meeting; a partner that fails two consecutive next actions drops one tier.

### 5.5 Wording rules for incumbent and donor outreach (mDoc, Jacaranda, MSD for Mothers)

## 4. Problem

- **UK.** Public inquiries into maternity services (Ockenden 2022; Kirkup 2022; the 2025–26 national investigation naming 14 trusts — `05` §1.1) repeatedly identify failures to listen to women, to escalate concerns and to hand over between professionals and settings. MBRRACE-UK's 2025 report (deaths 2021–23) gives a maternal death rate of 12.82 per 100,000 maternities and finds Black women more than twice as likely to die as White women, with Asian women also at elevated risk (source and URL in `05` §1.1; cite the report itself in any external deck). England and Wales recorded 594,677 live births in 2024 (ONS, cited in `05` §1.1).
- **Nigeria.** The modelled maternal mortality ratio in 2023 was 993 per 100,000 live births, the highest in Africa (WHO/World Bank series via the compilation cited in `05` §2; TO VALIDATE against the primary report before external use). Nigeria is among the five countries with the largest absolute number of maternal deaths. The "three delays" — seeking, reaching and receiving care — are coordination failures, and connectivity gaps make offline-first design a requirement, not a feature.
- **The structural gap.** Care fails between people: between a woman and a changing set of professionals, between shifts, between community and hospital, and between a good idea and a safe, adopted product. Nobody owns that in-between space in either system.

## 5. Solution — MaternaLink (in development)

**Intended use (PROPOSED v1):** a care-coordination and communication platform for maternity services, designed to help women stay connected to their care team and help care teams hand over safely. It does not diagnose, predict, triage or score; it is designed to support, not replace, clinical judgement.

**v1 scope:** enrolment and consent · personalised contact schedules and reminders · clinically signed-off education in the woman's language · secure two-way messaging with a named human owner and an acknowledgement time · a self-recorded diary that is displayed, not interpreted · a care-team console · a structured handover summary · an escalation log · missed-contact lists · a programme dashboard. Channels: web app, SMS, WhatsApp, USSD and voice (roadmap v1.1–v1.2).

**Four gates before anything is sold:** IP documented · prototype audited · Clinical Safety Officer appointed and hazard log opened · DPIA completed (`04_MATERNALINK_PRODUCT_STRATEGY`).

**The research track (v3+):** the 2025 multimodal "Maternal Instability Score" concept is retained as R&D that advances only with data partnerships, ethics approval, clinical validation and a UK MDR / MHRA software-as-a-medical-device pathway. No clinical claim is made or implied before then.

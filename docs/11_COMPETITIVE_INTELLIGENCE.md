# 11 — VYTALIX COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE

**Owner:** Chief Strategy Officer · **Status:** PROPOSED v1 · **Date:** 8 September 2026 · **Governing file:** `/FACTS_BASE.md`. Every fact taken from the web carries its URL and the date it was read (8 Sept 2026 unless stated); anything without a URL is ESTIMATE, ASSUMPTION or TO VALIDATE. Pricing is stated only where public; otherwise "NOT PUBLICLY AVAILABLE". Vytalix has no product in production, no customers and no evidence today (KNOWN); MaternaLink (in development) is compared as a *proposed* non-diagnostic care-coordination, communication, education and monitoring-support platform (FACTS_BASE A4), not as the 2025 AI concept.

## Executive view

1. **No competitor occupies the exact position Vytalix proposes — a non-diagnostic, woman-facing coordination and handover layer that sits beside the maternity record, designed for both NHS trusts and low-connectivity African programmes — but every adjacent position is occupied by someone better funded.** US virtual-maternity leaders (Maven, Babyscripts, Bloomlife) have raised $37m–$425m+; UK maternity records are a three-vendor oligopoly (System C BadgerNet, K2 Athena, Magentus Euroking); African maternal messaging is dominated by donor-funded programmes that cost under $1 per mother (Jacaranda PROMPTS, MomConnect) or by mDoc's MSD-for-Mothers-funded Digital Mom Project in the very state MaternaLink proposed to enter (VERIFIED).
2. **The most important competitive fact for Vytalix is a price anchor, not a product**: PROMPTS reports 2.8m+ women at "less than $1 per mother", and MomConnect ran nationally on SMS/WhatsApp. Any per-woman price above a few pounds must buy something messaging does not — facility-side coordination, handover, CHEW workflow, evaluation-grade data — and the £300/woman/year in the Ekiti proposal is indefensible against this benchmark (FACTS_BASE A5).
3. **The UK product threat is not a start-up; it is the EPR vendors adding patient-facing modules** (BadgerNet's patient app footprint, K2's woman-facing features — scope TO VALIDATE) and the free charity app Baby Buddy. MaternaLink's UK case must therefore be "integration-ready, vendor-neutral, evidence-generating, built with community organisations", not "better app".
4. **In services, Vytalix competes with firms it should also sub-contract to**: Carnall Farrar, PA Consulting and Deloitte in the NHS; Dalberg and Genesis Analytics in African health systems; Health Innovation Networks as quasi-competitors that are also the best route to trusts. The realistic boutique position is speed, fixed price, founder delivery and the UK–Africa corridor — a positioning that becomes usable only when the founder's credentials and first engagements support it (TO VALIDATE).
5. **Advantages Vytalix can realistically build in 24 months (§6): a vendor-neutral coordination layer with documented safety artefacts; a community-co-designed, multilingual content set; an honest evidence programme; a partner-first Africa model; and a services practice that feeds the product. Advantages it cannot claim today: any clinical outcome, any validated AI, any NHS or government relationship, any cost advantage over donor-funded incumbents, and any regulatory status.**

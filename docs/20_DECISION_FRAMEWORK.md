## Executive view
1. One scoring sheet for every idea that competes for the founder's time or the company's cash: ten criteria, each 1–10, unweighted, total out of 100, with three criteria reverse-scored (cost, regulatory risk, execution complexity — a high score means low cost, low risk, low complexity) and four knock-out rules that override the total. Verdicts: **BUILD ≥ 70 · TEST 50–69 · DEFER 35–49 · KILL < 35.**
2. Applied to the eight live ideas today: **BUILD** founder-led advisory (76). **TEST** the research report as lead magnet (65), MaternaLink v1 care-coordination (59) and UK NHS pilot pursuit as a design-partner evaluation (54). **DEFER** e-learning launch in year 1 (49) and white-label MaternaLink for NGOs (45). **KILL** the MaternaLink AI risk score as a product now (33; knock-out on regulatory risk) and the Ekiti proposal at £300 per woman per year as written (33; knock-out on strategic fit and an unvalidated price). Every score is a desk ESTIMATE from the audit, not a forecast.
3. "KILL" means the idea as stated is not pursued; it does not mean the underlying opportunity is dead. The AI risk score re-enters as a v3 research track with a data partner and an ethics route; the Ekiti conversation re-enters as a partner-aware, re-priced option scored at gate G-D on Day 60 (Thu 12 Nov 2026).
4. "TEST" is a commitment to a defined experiment with a budget cap, a KPI and a decision date — not a soft yes. The three TEST verdicts already have their experiments in the 90-day plan (report 1 by Week 9–10; PRD sign-off and the MVP build decision on Day 86; 15 UK discovery interviews and five design-partner briefs by Day 90).
5. Re-score quarterly (first: week of Mon 14 December 2026) and at every gate; any score that moves by three or more points must cite new evidence. A blank template (§6) is provided for future ideas; scoring without evidence labels is not accepted.

## 1. The ten criteria

| # | Criterion | What it measures | Direction | 1 means | 10 means | Evidence that raises confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strategic fit | Alignment with the group thesis: services fund the company, MaternaLink (in development) is the equity story, UK-first then Nigeria, non-diagnostic v1, truth-first | Normal | Contradicts the thesis or the language rules | Directly advances the thesis and at least two pillars | Business model §1; FACTS_BASE A4–A6 |
| 2 | Revenue potential | Realistic cash in 12–24 months and the size of the stream at maturity, probability-weighted | Normal | No path to cash | Large, recurring, high-margin, evidenced | Signed engagements; validated pricing; comparable programmes |
| 3 | Customer demand | Evidence that a named buyer wants this and can pay | Normal | No buyer conversation; no public evidence | Multiple paying customers or signed LOIs | Discovery calls; LOIs; public procurement signals |
| 4 | Speed to market | Time to first paying customer or first live use | Normal | > 24 months | < 1 month | Dependency chain in the 90-day plan |
| 5 | Cost | Cash required to reach the first result (**reverse-scored**) | Reverse | > £250k before first result | < £2k | Budget lines in the 90-day plan and financial model |
| 6 | Scalability | Growth without proportional founder time or cost | Normal | Founder-hours only | Software-like margins across markets | Unit economics in the business model |
| 7 | Competitive advantage | Defensible difference versus named alternatives, buildable within 12 months | Normal | Commodity; incumbents stronger on every axis | Unique, protected, evidenced | Competitive intelligence doc |
| 8 | Regulatory risk | Exposure to medical-device, data-protection, clinical-safety, procurement and financial-promotion rules (**reverse-scored**) | Reverse | Requires a claim or pathway Vytalix cannot lawfully make or fund now | No regulatory exposure beyond ordinary B2B | Legal and regulatory doc §2–§6 |
| 9 | Execution complexity | Number of dependencies, partners, skills and unknowns between now and the first result (**reverse-scored**) | Reverse | Many external dependencies, several unknowns, no owner | One owner, known steps, no external dependency | Task dependencies in the 90-day plan |
| 10 | Founder leverage | Whether the solo founder can deliver it with current skills, time and network without hiring first | Normal | Requires a team the company does not have | Founder can deliver alone within the time-allocation rule | Founder OS §1; hiring plan triggers |

**Reverse-scoring rule.** Criteria 5, 8 and 9 are assessed as High / Medium / Low first, then scored so that Low = 8–10, Medium = 4–7, High = 1–3. This keeps every criterion pointing the same way: a higher total is always better, and a reader can compare ideas by a single number.

**Evidence labels.** Each score carries one of KNOWN / VERIFIED / ASSUMPTION / ESTIMATE / TO VALIDATE. A score of 8 or above on criteria 2, 3 or 7 requires at least one KNOWN or VERIFIED item in the evidence column; without it the score is capped at 7.

## 2. Thresholds and verdicts

| Verdict | Total /100 | Meaning | What happens next | Rationale for the band |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BUILD** | ≥ 70 | Commit founder time and budget; put it in the operating plan with owner, dates and KPIs | Enters the 90-day plan as P0/P1 tasks; reported weekly | An average of 7 across all ten criteria means the idea is strong on nearly every dimension and weak on none that matters; with unweighted criteria this is the level at which a pre-seed company can commit scarce time without hedging |
| **TEST** | 50–69 | Promising but with real unknowns; run a bounded experiment | A written test card: hypothesis, budget cap (£ ESTIMATE), KPI, decision date; re-scored on that date | The mid-band is where most product ideas sit before validation; the right response is to buy evidence cheaply, not to commit or abandon |
| **DEFER** | 35–49 | Not now; revisit when a named trigger fires | Logged with the trigger (e.g., "after MVP", "after first NHS design partner", "Month 9"); no time spent beyond a waitlist or a note | Below the midpoint the idea fails on several criteria at once; spending time now would starve a BUILD or TEST item |
| **KILL** | < 35 | Not pursued as stated | Recorded with the reason; the underlying opportunity may re-enter as a differently stated idea | An average under 3.5 means the idea fails on most dimensions; carrying it forward costs credibility with investors and buyers |

**Knock-out rules (override the total):**
- **K1 — Strategic fit ≤ 3 → KILL** regardless of total. An idea that contradicts the thesis or the language rules is not improved by scoring well elsewhere.
- **K2 — Regulatory risk ≤ 2 → cannot be BUILD or TEST.** Maximum verdict DEFER until a lawful, funded pathway exists; if the idea requires a claim prohibited by `/FACTS_BASE.md` §4 it is KILL as stated.
- **K3 — Founder leverage ≤ 2 with no funded team → cap at DEFER.** The company has one operator; an idea that needs a team the company cannot pay for waits for the round or the revenue.
- **K4 — Customer demand ≤ 2 → cannot be BUILD.** Nothing is built without at least one buyer conversation that says so.

**Tie-breaks and adjustments:** if two ideas compete for the same founder hours, the one that creates revenue sooner (criterion 4 × criterion 2) wins; if a score sits on a band boundary, the verdict takes the lower band until evidence moves it.

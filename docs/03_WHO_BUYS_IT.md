# 03 — Who buys it, and who else is out there

**This document tells you who will pay you money, who you call first, and who you are up against.**

Labels used below: **ESTIMATE** (our best guess, not a fact), **KNOWN** (checked and true today), **TO VALIDATE** (must be tested before you say it out loud).

## In one minute

- Vytalix has no customers, no pilots and no partners today (KNOWN). Everything here is a plan.
- Only three kinds of buyer can pay you within 90 days: health-tech companies, NGOs with a live grant, and universities or accelerators.
- The NHS, pharma and African governments are worth talking to now, but they pay in 2027 or later.
- MaternaLink is bought by hospitals, NGOs and clinics. It is used by mothers and midwives. Those are different people.
- Nobody sits exactly where you want to sit. But every neighbouring position is taken by someone with more money.

## Words explained

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| EPR | Electronic patient record: the hospital's main computer system holding a woman's notes |
| DTAC | The NHS checklist a digital product must pass before a hospital can buy it |
| DCB0160 / DCB0129 | The NHS safety paperwork: one for the hospital using a system, one for the company making it |
| CQC | The regulator that inspects and rates hospitals in England |
| ICB / LMNS | The regional NHS body that plans care, and its maternity planning group |
| HMO | A Nigerian health insurer that also runs its own care |
| MHRA | The UK medicines and medical devices regulator |
| CRM | The spreadsheet or tool where you track every account and every next step |

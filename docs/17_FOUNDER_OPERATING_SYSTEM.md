# 17 — VYTALIX FOUNDER OPERATING SYSTEM

**Owner:** Founder (CEO) · **Status:** PROPOSED · **Version:** 1.0 (8 September 2026) · **Governed by:** `/FACTS_BASE.md` · **Feeds:** `/operations/KPI_DASHBOARD.csv`, `/operations/PIPELINE_TRACKERS.csv` · **Related:** `/docs/16_ORGANISATION_AND_SOPS.md` (SOP 11 Reporting), `/docs/18_90_DAY_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`, `/docs/13_SALES_ENGINE.md`, `/docs/14_MARKETING_AND_CONTENT_ENGINE.md`, `/investors/OUTREACH_ENGINE.md`

## Executive view
1. Vytalix has one operator and no operating rhythm (VERIFIED absence, master audit §2 "Operating system: 0"). This document is that rhythm: a fixed daily shape, a Monday plan, a Friday review, a monthly close and re-score, and a quarterly strategy reset — designed so that revenue work happens before anything else every day.
2. The founder's week is budgeted, not improvised: 50 working hours, of which ≥ 35 (70%) are Vytalix; within Vytalix, 40% revenue, 20% investors, 20% product, 20% partnerships/credibility/infrastructure in the first 90 days. Kemek Enterprise Ltd is a declared conflict capped at 30% and reported weekly.
3. Two dashboards, one source of truth. The **CEO dashboard** is 12 numbers on one page read every Monday; the **KPI dashboard** is 34 metrics across Commercial, Investment, Product, Marketing and Finance with written formulas and Month 1–3 targets, stored in `/operations/KPI_DASHBOARD.csv`. Every value carries a label (ACTUAL / TARGET / ESTIMATE); today every ACTUAL is zero.
4. Five pipeline trackers (investor, partnership, product, revenue, strategic projects) share one CSV so that the Friday review is a 45-minute exercise, not a half-day. Example rows are marked EXAMPLE and are not records of anything that has happened.
5. The weekly review answers five questions in order — what creates revenue, what creates investor interest, what advances the product, what advances partnerships, what needs my attention — and ends with next week's three commitments. Month 1–3 targets are PROPOSED and taken from the sales, marketing and business-model documents; none is a forecast.

## 2. Rituals

### 2.1 Daily (Monday–Friday)

| Time | Block | What happens | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 08:00–08:30 | Start-up | Read CEO dashboard (Mon) or yesterday's numbers; pick the day's three priorities (one must be revenue) | 3 priorities written in the daily log |
| 08:30–11:00 | Revenue block | Outreach (8 new contacts/day), follow-ups, discovery calls, proposals, delivery on signed work | CRM updated; calls held |
| 11:00–11:15 | Break | — | — |
| 11:15–12:45 | Priority block | Whatever the week's plan assigns (investor prep, product gates, partnerships, legal/admin) | Task moved to done |
| 12:45–13:30 | Lunch and LinkedIn | Post (Mon/Wed/Fri) or comment on 5 posts (Tue/Thu); reply to DMs | Engagement logged |
| 13:30–15:30 | Meetings window | External calls and meetings batched here | Notes in CRM within 30 minutes |
| 15:30–17:00 | Second deep-work block | Delivery, writing, product/clinical documents | Deliverable progress |
| 17:00–17:30 | Shutdown | Log activities, update next actions, tomorrow's three priorities; Kemek work only after this | Daily log closed |

Daily non-negotiables: 8 new contacts, 5 follow-ups, 1 discovery call or meeting attempt, every CRM record touched has a next action date, zero unanswered inbound older than 24 hours.

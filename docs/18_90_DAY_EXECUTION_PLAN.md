# 18 — VYTALIX 90-DAY EXECUTION PLAN (Mon 14 September – Sat 12 December 2026)

**Owner:** Founder (CEO/COO) · **Status:** PROPOSED · **Version:** 1.0 (8 September 2026) · **Day 1 = Monday 14 September 2026** · **Governed by:** `/FACTS_BASE.md` · **Related:** `/docs/00_MASTER_AUDIT.md` (problems and immediate actions), `/docs/16_ORGANISATION_AND_SOPS.md` (SOPs referenced by number), `/docs/17_FOUNDER_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md` (rituals and KPI definitions), `/docs/19_FIRST_30_DAYS.md` (day-by-day detail for Days 1–30), `/docs/20_DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md` (scoring used at the gates), `/investors/` (CRM, call lists, outreach engine)

## Executive view
1. Ninety days to move Vytalix from an audit score of 11/100 (pre-formation) to 35–40 (formed, trading, evidenced enough to open investor conversations honestly). Four phases: **Foundation** (Days 1–7), **Commercial infrastructure** (Days 8–30), **Investor acquisition and market validation** (Days 31–60), **Product and commercial launch** (Days 61–90).
2. Four decision gates control the plan: **Incorporate** (decide Day 1, execute by Day 12), **IP resolution** with David Agunede (heads of terms Day 7, signed Day 30, hard stop Day 60), **MaternaLink re-scope** (adopt the non-diagnostic v1 intended-use statement Day 3), **Ekiti go/no-go** (Day 60, after a status call, an mDoc conversation and a two-state scorecard). No investor outreach begins until gates 1–3 are closed — the pre-conditions in `/investors/OUTREACH_ENGINE.md`.
3. Revenue is the first priority throughout: the sales quota (40 contacts, 20 conversations, 5 discovery calls, 2 proposals per week) starts Day 8, the first signed engagement is due Day 30, three by Day 90, £15k–£30k contracted (ESTIMATE range, not a forecast).
4. The investor phase uses the assets already built in `/investors/`: 253 researched investors, a Top 25 with per-investor scripts, four call lists and an 18-stage pipeline. Target by Day 90: 125 contacted, 10 meetings, 2 active discussions, one grant bid submitted — with £0 received assumed in the base case.
5. Cash cost of the 90 days is £9k–£19k (ESTIMATE: legal, trademark, incorporation, insurance, tooling, CSO, prototype audit, report design), against £15k–£30k contracted revenue if the sales targets hold. 62 actions are listed with owner, calendar deadline, dependency, expected outcome, KPI and priority (P0 = the plan fails without it; P1 = the phase fails without it; P2 = valuable, movable).

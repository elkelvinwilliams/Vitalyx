**Owner:** Founder (acting COO) · **Status:** PROPOSED · **Version:** 1.0 (8 September 2026) · **Governed by:** `/FACTS_BASE.md` (language rules apply to every SOP output) · **Related:** `/docs/02_GROUP_STRUCTURE.md` (governance, advisory board, RACI), `/docs/13_SALES_ENGINE.md`, `/docs/14_MARKETING_AND_CONTENT_ENGINE.md`, `/docs/15_LEGAL_AND_REGULATORY.md`, `/docs/17_FOUNDER_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`, `/docs/18_90_DAY_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`

## Executive view
1. Vytalix today is one person, no entity, no staff and one informal collaborator whose role is undefined (VERIFIED, FACTS_BASE §1). The organisation design below is therefore a **founder-led company with a bought-in bench**: twelve functions, of which nine are founder-led for the first six months, three are outsourced from Day 1 (legal, accounting, clinical safety), and none is a salaried hire before a signed engagement or a closed round pays for it.
2. Every hire in the 24-month plan is tied to a **trigger** (revenue, funded milestone or gate), never to a calendar date. Indicative fully loaded cost of the year-1 bench is £28k–£62k (ESTIMATE), rising to £310k–£520k in year 2 only if the MaternaLink (in development) MVP is funded (ESTIMATE; see §3).
3. Eleven SOPs cover the loop that turns founder time into cash, capital and product: business development, investor acquisition, fundraising, product development, customer acquisition, customer onboarding, healthcare partnerships, due diligence, compliance, finance and reporting. Each follows one format — Trigger → Owner → Process → Documents → Approval → KPI → Output — so a future hire, contractor or virtual assistant can run it without the founder.
4. The SOPs enforce the three non-negotiables from the audit: no MaternaLink claim beyond "in development" (claims sign-off is a step in every external-facing SOP), no outreach to investors before the four pre-conditions in `/investors/OUTREACH_ENGINE.md` are met, and no processing of patient data without a DPIA and Clinical Safety Officer sign-off.
5. Until incorporation, "Approval" in every SOP resolves to the founder alone, minuted in a decision log (`/operations/DECISION_LOG.md`, TO CREATE Day 1). From the first board meeting (target Tue 13 Oct 2026), reserved matters in `/docs/02_GROUP_STRUCTURE.md` §4.1 pass to the board.

## 1. Design principles

| # | Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue funds people | No salaried hire until either (a) trailing 3-month services revenue ≥ £10k/month, or (b) a round or grant that names the role has closed. |
| 2 | Buy expertise, hire execution | Clinical safety, legal, accounting, regulatory and design are bought by the day; the first hires are people who ship (engineer, delivery associate, VA). |
| 3 | One owner per function | Every function in §2 has exactly one accountable person at all times, even when that is the founder for nine functions at once. |
| 4 | Contractor IP always assigned | Every contractor, associate, adviser and developer signs the IP clause in `/docs/15_LEGAL_AND_REGULATORY.md` §9.1 before work starts. No exceptions, including David Agunede. |
| 5 | Nigeria delivered locally | In-country roles (country lead, CHEW trainers, engineers) are contracted in Nigeria through a local partner or a Nigerian employer-of-record, not relocated from the UK (TO VALIDATE employment route). |
| 6 | Founder time is the scarcest asset | Founder time allocation rule: ≥ 70% Vytalix; within that, revenue 40% / investors 20% / product 20% / partnerships and credibility 20% in the first 90 days (see `/docs/17_FOUNDER_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`). Kemek Enterprise Ltd is a declared conflict and gets ≤ 30%. |

## 4. Standard operating procedures

Format for all eleven: **Trigger → Owner → Process (numbered) → Documents → Approval → KPI → Output.** All templates referenced live in `/operations/` (TO CREATE where marked) or in the named docs. Every SOP that produces external material includes a FACTS_BASE language check as a numbered step.

### SOP 01 — Business development (Advisory / Consulting)

**Trigger:** Weekly (Monday 08:30 planning block) and on any inbound enquiry.
**Owner:** Founder (acting Head of Sales). Delegate lead research and CRM logging to VA when engaged.
**Process:**
1. Pull the ICP target list (`/docs/13_SALES_ENGINE.md` §1, segments S1–S2 first) and confirm the weekly quota: 40 new contacts, 20 conversations, 5 discovery calls, 2 proposals (steady state from Week 3).
2. Research each new contact: role, organisation, funding stage, stated problem, public email/LinkedIn only; record the source URL in the CRM (HubSpot Free).
3. Score the lead (sales doc §4); A ≥ 70 = call within 48 h; B = weekly nurture; C = monthly content.
4. Send first touch using template E1/L1 with one personalised reason; log the activity the same day.
5. Follow up on days 4, 10 and 21; three touches without reply = "Closed-lost: no decision", not deleted.
6. On a reply, book a 30-minute discovery call within 5 working days; send the discovery agenda.
7. Run the discovery call to the 30-minute script (sales doc §5); capture Problem / Budget / Authority / Timeline / Success criteria in the CRM.
8. Within 48 h decide: proposal, second call, refer out, or close-lost with a coded reason.
9. Draft the proposal from the package menu (`/docs/01_BUSINESS_MODEL.md` §4.1: A1, A2, C1–C8, T1) with fixed price, scope, start date and 14-day validity.
10. Run the FACTS_BASE language check on the proposal (no client claims, no MaternaLink capability claims).
11. Send; diarise follow-up at +3 and +7 days; move the deal stage.
12. Friday 30-minute pipeline clean: every open deal has a next action dated within 7 days.
**Documents:** ICP list; CRM (HubSpot); outreach templates E1–E3, L1–L2 (sales doc §7); discovery script; proposal template; SOW template (`/operations/SOW_TEMPLATE.md`, TO CREATE; solicitor review before first use); loss-reason codes.
**Approval:** Founder for proposals ≤ £25k; board (once formed) for any contract > £50k or any government proposal (`/docs/02_GROUP_STRUCTURE.md` §4.3).
**KPI:** contacts/week (40); conversations/week (20); discovery calls/week (5); proposals/week (2); reply rate ≥ 8% email, ≥ 15% LinkedIn; discovery→proposal ≥ 40%; proposal→win ≥ 25%; first signed engagement by Day 30, three by Day 90.
**Output:** A weekly pipeline snapshot in `/operations/PIPELINE_TRACKERS.csv`; proposals sent; signed SOWs handed to SOP 06.

### SOP 04 — Product development (MaternaLink (in development))

**Trigger:** Gate G0 (IP/ownership documented) passed and a funded build decision, **or** any request to change scope, intended use or a feature.
**Owner:** Founder as acting CPO until a fractional product lead is engaged; fractional CTO for technical decisions; CSO for safety.
**Process:**
1. Confirm gates before any sprint: G0 IP deed; prototype audit complete (`/docs/04_MATERNALINK_PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md` §25); CSO engaged and hazard log open; DPIA v0.1 started; intended-use statement adopted (§21.1).
2. Work from `/product/MATERNALINK_PRD.md` (118 requirements, MoSCoW) and `/product/MATERNALINK_MVP_SPEC.md`; no requirement is added without a PRD change record.
3. Run two-week sprints: planning (Monday), daily 15-minute stand-up (async acceptable), review and retro (Friday of week 2); founder attends review only.
4. Every feature passes the **line-crossing check**: does it interpret, score, alert on, or triage clinical information? If yes, it is out of v1 and logged for the G8 qualification decision.
5. Every user-facing content item passes clinical sign-off (advisory board #1/#2 or CSO) before release; log the sign-off ID.
6. Hazard log (DCB0129) updated at each sprint review; new hazards assessed by the CSO within 5 working days.
7. Security controls per MVP spec (audit logging, encryption, secrets management, dependency scanning) verified in the definition of done; no real patient data in any non-production environment.
8. Discovery loop: ≥ 20 women and ≥ 10 professionals per market interviewed before MVP freeze (strategy §3.2); findings logged against personas.
9. Release: staging → penetration test (before go-live and after major change) → safety-case sign-off → production; release notes in plain English.
10. Monthly: report features shipped, users, activation and retention to the Product section of the KPI dashboard.
**Documents:** PRD; MVP spec; roadmap (`/product/MATERNALINK_ROADMAP.md`); intended-use statement; hazard log and clinical safety case; DPIA; sprint board; release notes; PRD change records.
**Approval:** Intended-use or scope changes — board (reserved matter) with CSO advice; feature release — CSO sign-off on safety case + founder; content — clinical adviser sign-off.
**KPI:** sprint commitment delivered ≥ 80%; open critical hazards at release = 0; critical/high vulnerabilities open > 14 days = 0; interviews completed (20 women / 10 professionals per market); milestone dates met (MVP internal alpha Q1 2027 if funded, ESTIMATE).
**Output:** Shippable increments; updated hazard log and safety case; DTAC readiness pack; monthly product KPI row.

### SOP 06 — Customer onboarding (signature to first value)

**Trigger:** Signed SOW received.
**Owner:** Founder (delivery lead); VA for logistics; associate if assigned.
**Process:**
1. Within 24 h: create the engagement folder (`/clients/<code>/`, private), add SOW, contacts, success criteria and the deliverable schedule.
2. Within 3 working days: 45-minute kick-off — confirm scope, deliverables, dates, counterpart, decision-maker, communication cadence (weekly 30-minute check-in), and what "done" looks like in their words.
3. Send the kick-off note within 24 h (scope confirmation, timeline, risks, next actions); this is the baseline for change control.
4. Set up the working tools: shared folder, meeting series, NDA if not already in the MSA; confirm no personal or patient data will be shared (or trigger SOP 09 if it must be).
5. Deliver the first tangible output within 10 working days (discovery interviews summary, framework, or draft outline) — first value fast.
6. Weekly check-in: progress, blockers, decisions needed; log in the engagement file.
7. Mid-point review (for engagements ≥ 3 weeks): scope drift check; change request if needed.

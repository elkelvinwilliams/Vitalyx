# VYTALIX INVESTOR OUTREACH ENGINE

Companion to `VYTALIX_INVESTOR_CRM.xlsx`, `TOP_25_INVESTORS.md`, `CALL_LISTS.md` and the per-investor scripts in `OUTREACH_TOP25.md`. Pre-conditions before ANY outreach: (1) Vytalix incorporated, (2) MaternaLink IP heads of terms signed, (3) the 2025 clinical-AI claims retired from all materials, (4) one-pager and data-room v1 ready, (5) SEIS/EIS advance assurance submitted (say "applied for", never "approved", until it is).

## 1. Operating rules
- Phone-first for A-tier with a verified number; email/LinkedIn-first otherwise. Never call personal mobiles. Log every touch in `CRM Status` the same day.
- One personalised reason per message: a named portfolio company, a stated thesis, or a public statement by the partner. No mass messages.
- Truth rule: describe stage as "pre-seed, founder-led, prototype and pilot in design". Never imply NHS, government or clinical validation.
- Cadence: call → same-day email → +4 days follow-up #1 → +10 days follow-up #2 → +21 days follow-up #3 → quarterly update list. Three touches without response = "Future Opportunity", not "Passed".
- Weekly volume target (founder, weeks 5–12): 15 calls, 25 emails, 20 LinkedIn touches, 3 meetings.

## 2. Phone opening (generic, 25 seconds)
"Good morning, [name], this is [Founder], founder of Vytalix, a UK health-technology group. I'm calling because [Fund] backed [portfolio company] and we're building something in an adjacent space: MaternaLink, a maternal-health coordination platform in development for the UK and Nigeria. I'm not asking for a decision on this call. I'd like 20 minutes with [partner] to test fit against your [stage] mandate. Is [partner] the right person, and what's the best way to get that slot?"

Gatekeeper variant: "Could you tell me who leads health-tech investments and the best route to reach them, an email or a pitch form? I'll keep it to one page."

Voicemail (15 seconds): name, company, one-line reason, "I'll send a one-page summary by email today; my number is [number]."

## 3. Email (generic skeleton)
Subject: Maternal-health platform (UK + Nigeria), 20 minutes?
1. Who you are, one line. 2. Why them specifically, one line with a named portfolio company or thesis. 3. What exists today, honestly, three bullets. 4. What you're raising and for what, one line. 5. The ask: 20 minutes, one-pager attached. Under 150 words.

## 4. LinkedIn
Connection note under 300 characters with the specific reason. After acceptance, one message with the one-line pitch and the 20-minute ask. Do not pitch in the connection note beyond one sentence. Comment on the partner's recent post once before messaging if possible.

## 5. Follow-ups
- #1 (day 4): one-pager link plus one genuine question about their process or thesis.
- #2 (day 10): one new proof point (advisor confirmed, pilot LOI, first paying client, grant submitted). No proof point, no follow-up: create one first.
- #3 (day 21): close the loop; ask for a referral to a better-fit fund; move to quarterly update.

## 6. Investor meeting structure (30 minutes)
| Min | Segment | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Frame | Thank, confirm time, agree agenda, ask what they want to leave with. |
| 3–8 | Their mandate | Stage, ticket, process, what they've passed on recently in health-tech and why. |
| 8–12 | Problem and why now | UK maternal inequality evidence; Nigeria burden; communication and coordination failures cited in national reviews. Cite sources. |
| 12–17 | What exists | Prototype, government conversation status, services revenue (actuals only), team and advisors (real only). |
| 17–22 | Product and evidence plan | MaternaLink v1 (non-diagnostic), pilot design, what claims each evidence stage permits, regulatory posture. |
| 22–26 | Business model and round | Services fund the company; platform is the equity story; round size, use of funds, milestones. |
| 26–29 | Their objection | Ask directly: "What would stop you?" Answer it or commit to a written answer within 48 hours. |
| 29–30 | Next step | Agree a concrete next step with a date. |

## 7. Close: from interest to investment discussion
1. Interest signal → send data room (one-pager, deck, financial model summary, IP agreement summary, advisory board, pilot plan, risk register).
2. Second meeting with a partner → present the 90-day milestone plan; ask for their diligence list.
3. Diligence → answer within 48 hours; keep a Q&A log; introduce advisors.
4. Term discussion → SEIS/EIS-compatible instrument (advance subscription agreement or priced round; solicitor to advise); target lead first, then fill.
5. Close → signed docs, funds, Companies House filings, SEIS/EIS compliance statements.

## 8. Objection handling (investors)
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Too early." | Agree. Ask what milestones would make it right, book a 90-day update, ask for one intro. |
| "Solo founder." | Show the advisory board plan and fractional CTO/clinical leads; state the hiring plan tied to the round. |
| "Government sales in Nigeria are slow and risky." | Agree; Nigeria is a second market; UK services and pilots come first; government revenue is upside, not base case. |
| "Is this a medical device?" | v1 is designed to stay non-diagnostic with a documented intended-use statement; predictive features only via a validated SaMD pathway. |
| "mDoc is already there." | Correct; we differentiate on facility-side coordination and interoperability, and target different states or NGO channels; partnership is an option. |
| "What do you own?" | The IP agreement (once signed). Until then, do not pitch. |
| "Why will hospitals pay?" | Pilot-first, evidence-generating, priced to the budget-holder's problem (communication failures, DNA rates, complaints). Show the pricing logic, not a promise. |

## 9. Compliance for fundraising communications (UK)
Investment promotions are regulated. Use only communications that fall within FCA exemptions (e.g., to certified high-net-worth or self-certified sophisticated investors, or via an authorised platform) and get a solicitor to sign off the deck's disclaimers. Never say "guaranteed", "assured" or "low risk". TO VALIDATE with a solicitor before the first send.

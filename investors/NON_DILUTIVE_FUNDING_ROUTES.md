# NON-DILUTIVE FUNDING ROUTES (kept outside the investor CRM)

These are grant or contract funders, not investors, so they are excluded from the 300-investor CRM, the Top 25 and the call lists in line with the investor-only rule. They remain high priority for the funding strategy (see `/docs/09_FUNDING_STRATEGY.md`).

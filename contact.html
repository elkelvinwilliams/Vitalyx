What happens next

  1. We read every message personally; there is no sales team.
  2. You get a reply within five working days, even if the answer is "not us".
  3. If it makes sense to talk, we offer a 30-minute call at no charge.
  4. If there is work to do, you get a one-page brief or a written proposal before anything starts.

Pilots and partnerships. Choose "MaternaLink pilot or design partnership" and tell us your service, your role and your cohort. We will send our current assurance artefacts list and the draft evaluation approach before any call, so your governance team can see what they would be agreeing to.

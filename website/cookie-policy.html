1. What cookies and similar technologies are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores on your device so it can recognise you later. "Similar technologies" include local storage, which stores data in your browser without sending it back to a server on every request. The law treats both the same way: storing or accessing information on your device needs your consent unless it is strictly necessary for a service you asked for.

3. Google Fonts

The site's typefaces, Fraunces and Manrope, are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them it sends your IP address and standard browser information to Google, which is a transfer of personal data to Google's servers. Google states that it does not use the Fonts service to set cookies. Google's privacy policy applies to that request: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Hosting logs

Our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, page requested, browser type, time) to deliver the site and detect abuse. These are not cookies and are covered by the privacy notice. [Hosting provider and log retention — TO VALIDATE.]

5. If we add analytics later

We may in future want to know how many people read a page. If we do, our first choice is a cookieless, privacy-preserving measurement tool that sets no identifiers on your device, in which case this policy will be updated and no consent banner will be needed. If we ever add a tool that does set non-essential cookies or identifiers, we will ask for your consent first through a proper consent mechanism, and nothing non-essential will be set until you agree. We will not add advertising or social-media tracking.

6. Managing your choice

Your choice on the cookie notice is stored only in your browser. To clear it and see the notice again, use the button below. You can also block or delete local storage and cookies in your browser settings; doing so will not break this site.

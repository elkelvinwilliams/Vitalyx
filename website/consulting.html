Engagement models

How we contract, invoice and change scope

Contract
A short master services agreement plus a statement of work per package. Terms of business are supplied with the proposal. [Contract templates — to be reviewed by a solicitor before first use.]
Payment
Engagements under £20,000: 50% on signing, 50% on acceptance. Above £20,000: 40% on signing, 30% at the mid-point milestone, 30% on acceptance. 30-day terms.
Change control
Any change to scope, dates or deliverables is written down, priced and agreed by both sides before work continues. No surprises on the invoice.
Sub-contract
We work as a named sub-contractor to larger consultancies and Health Innovation Networks at founder day rates of £1,200–£1,800, indicative.
Associates
Where a package needs skills we do not hold — an NHS-experienced digital lead, an in-country associate, an evaluation methodologist — we name them in the proposal, with your consent, and never invent them.
Expenses
Remote by default. UK travel at cost with prior approval; international travel scoped separately.

Process

What happens after you get in touch

  1. Scoping call, 30 minutes. We ask twenty questions about the problem, the decision it feeds, who signs it off and when. If a package does not fit, we say so.
  2. Written proposal within five working days. One-page summary; context and objectives in your words; approach, including what we will not do; a deliverables table with acceptance criteria; timeline; commercials; assumptions and risks; terms.
  3. Kick-off and access. Named contacts on both sides, a shared working folder, an interview list, and a weekly 30-minute checkpoint booked for the whole engagement.
  4. Delivery with a mid-point review. Draft findings shared early enough to change course. Longer packages carry a milestone payment at this point.
  5. Acceptance and handover. Final deliverables walked through with you, acceptance criteria ticked, the reusable asset agreed, and a follow-up call booked six weeks out.

Patient data, by design, stays with you. Our consulting packages are designed to process no patient-identifiable data. Where a review genuinely needs it — a maternity outcomes data review, for instance — the analysis is done inside your environment by your staff or under a written data-processing agreement, with your information-governance lead's sign-off.

Honesty about proof. We have no completed engagements or references to show you yet. Every proposal includes a stage-honest one-pager about Vytalix so your governance team knows exactly who they are contracting with.

Culture

What it will be like to work here, stated before we have the people to prove it

These are commitments, written down now so that the first people who join can hold us to them.

Say what it is

Every proposal, product, course and job advert states its stage and limits in the first paragraph. Nobody is asked to write "trusted by" about a client we do not have.

Candour is the job

Telling a client "don't build it", or telling the founder "this feature crosses the device line", is what you are paid for. It is never a career risk.

Safety before speed

A Clinical Safety Officer can stop a release. So can a safeguarding concern. The roadmap has gates and we do not skip them for a demo.

Remote first, UK based, Nigeria connected

Work from where you work best. Meetings are short, written notes are long, and we assume colleagues in Lagos and London are equals.

Fixed scope, real hours

We sell fixed-price work so that people are not asked to absorb scope creep unpaid. The same rule applies inside the company.

Everyone teaches

Every engagement leaves a reusable asset. Everyone who makes one gets a byline on it when it is published.

The seven values behind these commitments are on the About page, each with a "never" attached.

How we will hire

Fairly, in writing, and never for free

  1. Roles are advertised here and on the company LinkedIn page with a salary or rate range, the basis (employed, contracted, fractional) and the stage of the company stated plainly. [LinkedIn company page URL — add when created.]
  2. No unpaid work. We do not run unpaid internships or "trial projects". Advisers have a written brief and are paid or granted options. The only exception is a formal, mutual, no-fee design partnership between organisations.
  3. Structured conversations. The same questions for every candidate for a role, a short written exercise where relevant, and feedback to everyone we speak to.
  4. Checks that the role needs and nothing more. Right to work, professional registration where the role requires it, and safeguarding checks where the role touches services. Your data is handled under our privacy notice and deleted when a process ends unless you ask us to keep it.

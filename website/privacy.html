3. Lawful bases

  • Replying to enquiries, considering partnerships and expressions of interest: our legitimate interests in running the business and responding to people who contact us (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We have assessed that this does not override your interests, because you chose to contact us and can ask us to stop at any time.
  • Waitlist and newsletter emails: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), given when you ask to be added. You can withdraw it in any email we send or by contacting us. We follow the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations for marketing email. [Solicitor to confirm treatment of corporate subscribers under PECR as amended.]
  • Business-to-business outreach: our legitimate interests in offering services to organisations (Article 6(1)(f)), documented in a legitimate-interests assessment. We contact people only in their professional capacity, about matters relevant to their role, and stop when asked.
  • Client, supplier and partner relationships: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and compliance with legal obligations such as tax and accounting (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Website technical logs: our legitimate interests in security and reliability (Article 6(1)(f)).

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to: give you a copy of your personal data (access); correct it (rectification); delete it (erasure); restrict how we use it; provide it in a portable form; and stop processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing (objection). Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting what was done before.

To exercise any right, use the contact form with the topic "Data protection request" or email hello@[domain — TO VALIDATE]. We respond within one month, extendable by two months for complex requests, and we may ask you to confirm your identity. There is no fee unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

8. Security

We keep the amount of personal data we hold small, use providers with recognised security certifications, protect accounts with multi-factor authentication, and limit access to the people who need it. If a breach is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours and, where the risk is high, notify you directly.

9. Children

This website and our services are aimed at professionals and organisations. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 through this site. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. MaternaLink and client engagements

MaternaLink is in development and does not process any personal data through this website. When it is deployed, the deploying health organisation will normally be the controller and Vytalix the processor under a data-processing agreement, with a data protection impact assessment completed first and a separate, plain-language privacy notice provided to women in their own language. Nothing about MaternaLink data is covered by this notice.

In advisory and consulting engagements we design the work to avoid patient-identifiable data. Where a client shares personal data with us, we act on the client's instructions as processor under the engagement contract, and the client's own privacy notice applies.

11. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when our processing changes, and we will show the version and date at the top. Material changes will be flagged on the home page for at least 30 days.

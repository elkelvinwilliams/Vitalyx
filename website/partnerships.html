We have no partners yet. Here is who we are looking for, and on what terms.

Vytalix is pre-formation. No partnership, pilot, licence or endorsement exists today, and none will be described on this site until it is signed. This page sets out the partnership models we intend to use and the kinds of organisation we hope to work with.

Partnership models

Six ways organisations can work with Vytalix

Each model has a written agreement behind it before anyone is called a partner. The word "partner" is reserved for a signed relationship; advisers, suppliers and friends of the company are described as what they are.

Design partner

A maternity service or programme that co-designs and evaluates MaternaLink (in development) under its own clinical governance, for no fee, with a written protocol, publication rights for both sides and termination for safety at any time.

Governed by a pilot or evaluation agreement with the DPIA and data-processing terms annexed.

Clinical academic partner

A research unit that designs and runs the service evaluation, then the feasibility study, and later — only under data-sharing agreements and ethics approval — the version-3 research track.

Collaboration agreement; joint grant applications where a call fits.

Implementing partner

An established NGO or state programme in Nigeria, and later one further African market, that delivers locally: training, community health worker supervision, state relationships, an existing cohort. Vytalix contracts through the partner until a signed pilot justifies a local entity.

Implementation agreement with data-residency and NDPA 2023 terms.

Content and standards partner

Organisations whose signed-off educational material, translations or standards we license or align to, with attribution and version control on every item.

Licence or alignment agreement; content governance rules apply.

Delivery and channel partner

Consultancies, Health Innovation Networks and programme organisations that co-deliver or sub-contract advisory and consulting work, at transparent rates and with conflicts declared.

Sub-contract or revenue-share agreement; referral fees always disclosed to the client.

Technology partner

Messaging gateways, hosting providers and record-system vendors whose services MaternaLink depends on, under processor contracts with no-training clauses where any model is involved.

Processor agreement under UK GDPR Article 28; international-transfer safeguards where relevant.

Principles

How we behave as a partner

  1. Written first, announced second. No organisation is named on this site, in a deck or in a bid without written consent and a signed agreement.
  2. Complement incumbents, do not fight them. Where a state or trust already has a programme, we look for the gap it leaves and propose to fill that, or we go elsewhere.
  3. No exclusivity we cannot honour. We do not offer exclusive territories or categories at this stage, and we say so up front.
  4. Conflicts on the table. Vytalix has a product in development and a founder with an unrelated business interest; both are declared in every partnership conversation.
  5. Publication whatever the result. Evaluation partners keep the right to publish negative findings. So do we.

What we will not do

  • Describe an adviser, supplier or contact as a partner
  • Claim NHS, government or professional-body endorsement through association
  • Pay or accept undisclosed referral fees; the UK Bribery Act applies to all our dealings, including in Nigeria
  • Sign a partnership that gives another party control of MaternaLink's intended-use statement or safety decisions

How we write

Four rules for anything published here

  1. Cite it or cut it. Every number has a source, checked on a stated date. If we cannot check it, we do not use it.
  2. Label the guesses. Estimates, assumptions and proposals are marked as such in the text, not buried in a footnote.
  3. Say what we do not know. Every longer piece ends with a section on open questions. It is the part most worth reading.
  4. No claims we cannot stand behind. Nothing here implies clients, partners, approvals or outcomes that Vytalix does not have.

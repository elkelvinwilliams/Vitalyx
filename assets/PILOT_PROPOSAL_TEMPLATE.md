## Notes for you, not for the buyer — delete before sending

- **Price from document 17.** Private £8k–15k Stage A, £15k–25k Stage B. NHS £5k–9k and £10k–18k.
- **Never send this without the company registered.** No company means no contract and no invoice.
- **Never remove the "what this does not include" section.** It is what keeps you legal and it is
  what makes a cautious clinical buyer trust you.
- **Do not discount.** Cut a week of scope instead and reprice.
- **Send it within 24 hours of the meeting.** Every day after that halves your chance.

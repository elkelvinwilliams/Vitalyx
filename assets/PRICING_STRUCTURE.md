# VYTALIX — Pricing Structure

**Version:** 1.0 · 8 September 2026 · Status: PROPOSED · Owner: Founder (acting CMO/BD)
Governed by `/FACTS_BASE.md`. All figures are ESTIMATE, in GBP, exclusive of VAT (VAT status TO VALIDATE), and consistent with `/docs/01_BUSINESS_MODEL.md` §2–§7 and `/docs/04_MATERNALINK_PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md` §9. Nothing here is a price we have ever charged; every price is a starting point to be tested in the first six proposals (`/docs/01_BUSINESS_MODEL.md` §8, item 2).

## Executive view

1. Services pricing is anchored on a founder day rate of £1,200–£1,800 (model £1,500) and an associate day rate of £700–£1,000; packages are priced as fixed fees derived from those days, never below the days they consume.
2. Retainers (£2,500–£6,000/month; standard £4,000 for two days) and the fractional officer role (£5,500–£8,000/month) are the recurring layer; they are capped at ~5 concurrent to protect delivery.
3. E-Learning pricing (£49–£299 per course UK; £10–£40 regional; £19/month subscription; corporate £2,500–£25,000) is set for month 9 launch and carries no accreditation claim.
4. MaternaLink (in development) is not for sale; the ranges here — UK pilot £20k–£60k, first paid pilot £10k–£40k, UK site licence £40k–£120k/yr, Africa platform £6–£40 per woman per year, NGO programme £8k–£60k/yr — exist to answer "roughly what would it cost?" honestly and to retire the unvalidated £300/woman/year figure.
5. Discount guardrails and payment terms are rules, not guidance: maximum 10% founder discretion, 15% for multi-package bundles, nothing below the day-rate floor, 50% on signing under £20k, 14-day terms.

## 1. Pricing principles

| Principle | Rule |
|---|---|
| Cost-to-serve first | Every price starts from founder/associate days at internal cost (founder opportunity cost £600/day, ASSUMPTION) plus direct costs, then a margin target of ≥55% on consulting and ≥70% on advisory |
| Fixed fee by default | Packages are fixed-fee, fixed-scope with written change control; time-and-materials is the fallback for undefined work |
| Value shown, not asserted | Proposals state the outcome the client can act on; no ROI guarantees |
| Three options | Good / Better / Best in every proposal; one recommended |
| Public "from" prices | The website shows "from" prices per package to qualify leads and shorten cycles |
| Test and record | Three price points tested in the first six proposals; objections coded in the CRM |
| Labelled | Every price is ESTIMATE and ex-VAT until the first invoice establishes an actual |

## 7. Discount guardrails

| Situation | Maximum discount | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Founder discretion (any reason) | 10% | Written reason in the CRM; never on the call |
| Multi-package bundle (two or more packages signed together) | 15% on the bundle | Scope fixed at signature; not stackable with other discounts |
| Retainer paid quarterly in advance | 5% | — |
| Charity, community organisation or NGO with no donor funding | Up to 20% or an "at-cost" day rate of £900 | One such engagement at a time; at-cost days capped at 10 per quarter |
| Pro bono | 100% | Capped at 5 founder days per quarter; a written scope; used for community-partner relationship-building (e.g. maternity voices organisations) |
| Sub-contract to a larger consultancy | Rate set by prime | Never below the £1,200 founder floor |
| Pricing change > 20% on any list price | Requires division P&L owner approval (`/docs/02_GROUP_STRUCTURE.md` §4.3) | Board informed |

Never: discount below the day-rate floor; trade a discount for a testimonial or logo (consent rules apply regardless); offer "free pilots" of MaternaLink outside the defined design-partnership model; discount E-Learning below regional pricing.

Concentration rule: no client above 40% of trailing six-month revenue by month 9 (`/docs/01_BUSINESS_MODEL.md` risks).

| Fixed-fee engagements under £20,000 | 50% on signature; 50% on acceptance of the final deliverable |
| Fixed-fee engagements of £20,000 or more | 40% on signature; 30% at interim readout; 30% on acceptance |
| Retainers and fractional roles | Monthly in advance |
| Advisory days | Invoiced on booking for single days; monthly in arrears for blocks under a retainer |
| Workshops and training | 100% on booking (cancellation: 50% within 14 days, 100% within 7 days) |
| E-Learning | Card payment at purchase; subscriptions monthly/annually in advance |
| MaternaLink pilots (when applicable) | 50% on signature, 50% at go-live; annual licences annually in advance; SMS pass-through monthly in arrears |
| Payment terms | 14 days from invoice; late-payment interest and compensation per the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 |
| Expenses | At cost with prior written approval; none unless stated in the SOW |
| Currency | GBP; overseas clients invoiced in GBP unless agreed; FX risk stated in the SOW |
| VAT | Prices exclude VAT; charged if/when registered (TO VALIDATE threshold and voluntary registration for B2B credibility) |
| Public-sector purchase orders | Accepted; work starts on PO receipt where 50% upfront is not possible |

Note: `/docs/13_SALES_ENGINE.md` §9 uses a £10k threshold for the 50%-upfront rule; this document and `/docs/01_BUSINESS_MODEL.md` §5 use £20k. Reconcile in the MSA at solicitor review (recommendation: £20k).

## Priorities / Risks / Next actions

**Priorities**
1. Test three price points (list, +15%, −10%) across the first six proposals and record objections and outcomes in the CRM.
2. Reconcile the 50%-upfront threshold (£10k vs £20k) in the MSA at solicitor review; publish "from" prices on the website package pages.
3. Retire the £300/woman/year figure from every conversation; use the §5 ranges and the £0.4m–£0.6m re-based programme illustration.

**Risks**
- Buyers anchor on associate-level rates for founder-led work; mitigation: the £1,200 floor and the repositioning rule.
- MaternaLink ranges are quoted as prices before the gates close; mitigation: §5 header language in every conversation; no written quote for MaternaLink before gate closure.
- Under-pricing packages that need contracted specialists (C3, C6, C8); mitigation: pass-through plus 15% coordination built into the fixed fee.

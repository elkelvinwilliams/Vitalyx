# VYTALIX — Sales Deck for a Services Prospect (10 slides)

**Audience:** a prospect for Advisory or Consulting after a discovery call (segments S1–S5 in `/docs/13_SALES_ENGINE.md` §1). **Length:** 10 slides, 20 minutes, following the presentation outline in `/docs/13_SALES_ENGINE.md` §6. **Version:** 1.0 · 8 September 2026 · Status: PROPOSED.
Design per `/docs/03_BRAND_SYSTEM.md` §13. Language per `/FACTS_BASE.md` §4. Bracketed fields are filled per prospect from the discovery notes (Problem / Impact / Authority / Budget / Timeline / Success criteria / Risks / Next step).

## Executive view

1. The deck follows one arc — problem → approach → packages → process → next step — and is customised from discovery notes, never sent cold.
2. It sells fixed-scope, fixed-price services that exist today; MaternaLink (in development) appears only on an optional slide when the prospect's problem is maternity-related.
3. Commercials are shown as three options (Good / Better / Best) built from the catalogue in `/assets/SERVICE_CATALOGUE.md` and priced per `/assets/PRICING_STRUCTURE.md`; all prices are ESTIMATE and exclusive of VAT.
4. Credibility is stage-honest: founder background from KNOWN facts, documented methods, no client logos, no testimonials, no invented associates.
5. The deck ends with a decision date and a kick-off date; the proposal (`/assets/PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md`) follows within two working days.

## Slide 4 — How we work

**Copy**
- Fixed scope, fixed price, written change control. You know the deliverable, the acceptance criterion and the date before we start.
- Founder-led. The person you met does the work; associates join only on signed work and only with your agreement.
- Evidence before claims. We cite sources; we tell you if the idea should not be built.
- Safety and data by design. Advisory and consulting engagements process no patient data.
- Every engagement leaves you a reusable asset — a template, a checklist, a model — not just a report.

**Speaker note:** three minutes; this is where a young firm earns trust — by being explicit about how it behaves rather than by claiming a track record.

**Visual:** five lines with monoline icons; whitespace; no imagery.

## Using this deck

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Never sent cold | Built from discovery notes; slide 2 must contain the prospect's own words |
| One recommendation | Slide 6 recommends one option; the others frame it |
| No claims that are not true | No logos, testimonials, "trusted by", named associates or advisers without consent; no MaternaLink outcome claims |
| Follow-up sequence | Same-day summary email; Day 2 proposal; Day 5 check-in call; Day 9 "what would help?"; Day 21 close (`/docs/13_SALES_ENGINE.md` §8) |
| Loss reasons coded | Price / timing / no budget / no need / chose competitor / no decision / not ICP — recorded in the CRM |

# VYTALIX 90-DAY LAUNCH CAMPAIGN

**Owner:** Founder (CMO role) · **Window:** Monday 14 September – Sunday 13 December 2026 · **Governing files:** `/FACTS_BASE.md`, `/docs/14_MARKETING_AND_CONTENT_ENGINE.md` (strategy and posts), `/assets/CONTENT_CALENDAR.csv` (dated items), `/docs/18_90_DAY_EXECUTION_PLAN.md` (gates)

## Executive view
1. For a pre-revenue company, "launch" means a credible presence and first real conversations, not claims. The campaign is built to be true on every day it runs.
2. Three outputs carry the campaign: a website that says exactly what exists, one published research report, and one webinar. Everything else is cadence.
3. Nothing external goes out before the name gate (Day 5) and the entity gate (Day 10); the company page and website wait for both.
4. Budget is lean: under £1,500 for the 90 days (ESTIMATE), almost all of it design and tooling. Founder time is the real cost: about six hours a week.
5. Success is measured in conversations, not vanity: 15 discovery calls, 3 clinician co-design conversations, 100 newsletter subscribers, 150 report downloads.

## 7. What "launch" means here
Vytalix will not announce customers, partners, approvals, outcomes or a team it does not have. The launch is the point at which a stranger can find Vytalix, understand exactly what it does today and what it is building, and start a conversation. That is enough for a company at this stage and it is the only launch that survives diligence.

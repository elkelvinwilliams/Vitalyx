**Owner:** Founder (acting CMO), with the Chief of Staff view · **Status:** PROPOSED · **Version:** 1.0 (8 September 2026) · **Governed by:** `/FACTS_BASE.md` §4 language rules and `/docs/03_BRAND_SYSTEM.md` voice rules · **Parent:** `/docs/14_MARKETING_AND_CONTENT_ENGINE.md` (strategy, pillars, channels, §5 week-by-week campaign, §7 post drafts, §8 article outlines, §9 report method, §10 webinar plan) · **Calendar:** `/assets/CONTENT_CALENDAR.csv` (every dated item in this campaign is a row there) · **Execution dependencies:** `/docs/18_90_DAY_EXECUTION_PLAN.md` gates G-A (incorporate), G-B (IP), G-C (re-scope) and `/docs/19_FIRST_30_DAYS.md` · **Sales hand-off:** `/docs/13_SALES_ENGINE.md`

## Executive view
1. For a pre-revenue, pre-formation company "launch" does not mean a product release, a press release or a claim. It means that by Sat 12 December 2026 Vytalix has a credible, stage-honest public presence (founder profile, company page, website, newsletter, one research report, one webinar) and that this presence has produced first conversations: 15 inbound conversations, 5 inbound-sourced discovery calls, 3 clinician conversations and a small investor audience who have watched the company being built (PROPOSED targets from `/docs/14_MARKETING_AND_CONTENT_ENGINE.md`; none is a forecast).
2. The campaign is founder-led and LinkedIn-first: 36 founder posts on a Tuesday–Thursday rhythm, 20 substantive comments a week, three long-form articles, five fortnightly newsletter issues, one report and one webinar. No paid media before Month 6. One channel done daily beats six done badly.
3. Every message obeys the stage-honest rule. Vytalix is "a new UK health-technology group, pre-seed and founder-led"; MaternaLink is "in development"; there are no clients, partners, approvals, pilots or outcomes to announce, and none is implied. Credibility is earned by showing thinking (decisions, sources, templates, mistakes) — the build-in-public pillar carries 20% of output for that reason.
4. The campaign is gated by the same decisions as the company: the company page waits for the name decision (Fri 18 Sept), the website waits for incorporation and legal pages (publish no later than Fri 9 Oct), the report and webinar wait for the interviews, and no adviser, clinician or client is named without written consent. If the name changes or the IP position is disputed, the campaign pauses under the rules in §10 rather than talking through it.
5. Cost: £1,300–£3,600 for the 90 days (ESTIMATE, lean: domain and tooling £0–£150 per month, report design £1k–£2k, event travel £150–£450, an optional freelance edit). Founder time is the real budget: roughly 8–10 hours a week inside the 20% "partnerships, credibility, infrastructure" allocation of the founder operating system, with content batched on Friday afternoons.

## 1. What "launch" means here — and what it does not

| Launch means (by Day 90) | Launch does not mean |
|---|---|
| A founder LinkedIn profile that states the stage honestly and publishes three times a week | A product release: MaternaLink (in development) has no release date and is not marketed to women or clinicians as available |
| A company page and a static website with services, "from" prices labelled ESTIMATE, an intended-use statement for MaternaLink (in development), an Insights page, legal pages and a newsletter sign-up | A press release announcing the company: PR is opportunistic and starts only with a real announcement (the report) |
| One published research report with a transparent, small-n method and a stated limitations section | Claims of clients, partners, NHS approval, government partnership, clinical validation, or outcomes |
| One delivered webinar with a recording and a follow-up sequence | Naming advisers, clinicians, design partners or customers without written consent |
| First conversations that the sales engine can work: inbound enquiries, discovery calls, clinician expressions of interest, investor follows | Investor outreach — that is the sales-and-investor engine, governed by its own pre-conditions from Day 31; marketing only makes the founder findable and credible when it starts |
| A measured baseline (followers, sessions, subscribers, downloads, conversations) from which Q1 2027 targets are set | Vanity reach: the only KPI that decides whether marketing worked is qualified conversations booked for the sales engine |

## 10. Governance and pause rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Claims check | Every post, article, page, newsletter, slide and pitch passes the `/FACTS_BASE.md` §4 check before scheduling; the Friday batch records "checked by / date" |
| Consent | No adviser, clinician, interviewee, client or partner is named, quoted or logo'd without written consent on file; the report anonymises all interviewees |
| Sources | Every statistic carries its source in the post or first comment; the MBRRACE-UK figure is re-checked against the current report before any use |
| Regulatory posts | Carry "a founder's summary, not legal advice"; A1, A2 and the webinar carry a disclaimer and a "when to hire a regulatory adviser" section |
| Financial promotions | No post, page or newsletter solicits investment; investor conversations happen in the investor engine under solicitor-approved wording (`/investors/OUTREACH_ENGINE.md` §9) |
| Data protection | ICO registration before the survey and the newsletter; double opt-in; privacy notice linked from every form; report download consent wording per `/docs/15_LEGAL_AND_REGULATORY.md` §2.8 |
| Pause triggers | (a) the name changes after the company page or site is live → 48-hour pause, rename, one honest post; (b) the IP position with David Agunede is disputed → all MaternaLink references reduced to "a concept Vytalix is developing with a collaborator" until resolved; (c) any external artefact found with a retired claim → removed the same day, logged, and the batch process reviewed; (d) a complaint from a clinician, patient group or regulator → pause the pillar concerned, respond within 24 hours, founder decides with the solicitor |
| Approval | Founder approves everything; from the first board meeting (Tue 13 Oct) the clinical-claims sign-off policy applies to anything that describes MaternaLink (in development) features |

# Vytalix — What we sell and what it costs

*For a buyer deciding whether to talk to us. Send it after a first conversation, before a proposal.*

We sell senior health-technology help in three forms: advice by the day or month, fixed-price projects, and training. One person leads every piece of work, and you always know the price before it starts.

Every price below is an ESTIMATE (our best guess, not a fact). We have not yet sold at these prices. We will hold a quoted price for 30 days once it is in a written proposal.

## The rules that apply to everything

- Fixed price and fixed scope. Any change is agreed in writing before it is done.
- Under £20,000: half on signing, half on finishing. Over £20,000: 40% on signing, 30% at the midpoint, 30% on finishing.
- Prices exclude VAT and travel. Travel is charged at cost and agreed in advance.
- We will tell you if a cheaper piece of work would answer your question.
- We do not take commission from any software vendor. We are paid by you only.

- Software resale. We take no commission from any vendor, ever.
- Anything about MaternaLink presented as available. MaternaLink is in development and is not for sale.
- Work where we would have a conflict of interest we have not told you about.

# VYTALIX — Service Catalogue

**Advisory · Consulting · E-Learning** · Version 1.0 · 8 September 2026 · Status: PROPOSED
Governed by `/FACTS_BASE.md`. Prices are consistent with `/docs/01_BUSINESS_MODEL.md` §4–§6 and `/assets/PRICING_STRUCTURE.md`; all are ESTIMATE, quoted exclusive of VAT (VAT status TO VALIDATE), and shown as "from" on the website. Every package is founder-led; associates are engaged only on signed work.

## Executive view

1. Three Advisory formats (day, retainer, fractional), ten fixed-price Consulting packages (C1–C10) and one training format are sellable now; four E-Learning products follow from month 9 (ESTIMATE) and are listed so that buyers can register interest.
2. Every package has a defined scope, named deliverables, a duration, an indicative price, an ideal buyer and the outcome it is designed to produce — fixed scope, fixed price, written change control.
3. Prices are anchored on a founder day rate of £1,200–£1,800 (model £1,500) and an associate day rate of £700–£1,000; a package is never priced below the founder days it consumes (`/docs/01_BUSINESS_MODEL.md` §3).
4. Some packages require capability that does not exist yet (a clinical adviser, a contracted Clinical Safety Officer, an in-country associate network); each is marked "Proof required to sell" and must not be sold until that is in place.
5. Advisory and consulting engagements are designed to process no patient data; anything involving patient data is routed to Vytalix Health Solutions and its safety and data-protection gates.

## 2. Vytalix Advisory

### A1 — Founder-led Advisory Day
- **Scope:** one day (on-site or remote) of senior, independent advice on a defined question: digital-health strategy, NHS or African market entry, regulatory pathway, pricing, investor or grant readiness, board preparation.
- **Deliverables:** pre-read questionnaire; the working session; a two-page written note within 48 hours with decisions, options and next steps.
- **Duration:** 1 day (booked in blocks of 1–5).
- **Indicative price:** £1,200–£1,800 per day (ESTIMATE; model uses £1,500); 1 founder day.
- **Who it is for:** any segment; most often S1 founders and S2 programmes needing a quick, candid second opinion.
- **Outcomes:** a clear answer or a clearly framed decision; the note is reusable with a board or investor.

### A2 — Advisory Retainer
- **Scope:** two days per month of advisory access — scheduled sessions, document review, introductions where appropriate, availability for short calls between sessions. Three-month minimum. No delivery work (that is a Consulting package).
- **Deliverables:** monthly agenda and written notes; quarterly written review of progress against the client's stated objectives.
- **Duration:** rolling monthly; 3–6-month minimum term; 30 days' notice.
- **Indicative price:** £4,000 per month for 2 days (ESTIMATE; range £2,500–£6,000 for 1.5–4 days); ~2.5 founder days including responsiveness.
- **Who it is for:** S1 health-tech founders (pre-seed to Series A), NGOs and SME providers; S2 accelerators for portfolio support.
- **Outcomes:** faster, better-informed decisions on go-to-market, regulation and funding; a documented decision trail.

### A3 — Fractional Chief Digital / Strategy Officer
- **Scope:** 4–6 embedded days per month acting as the organisation's senior digital or strategy lead: roadmap, vendor and procurement decisions, board papers, team coaching. Excludes line management and any clinical decision-making.
- **Deliverables:** a 30-day assessment; a 6-month roadmap; monthly board-level update; handover pack at exit.
- **Duration:** 6 months (renewable).
- **Indicative price:** £5,500–£8,000 per month (ESTIMATE); 4–6 founder days.
- **Who it is for:** scale-ups, private providers and NGOs (S1, S4, S5) that need senior capacity without a permanent hire.
- **Outcomes:** an executed roadmap rather than a report; organisational capability that outlasts the engagement.
- **Proof required to sell:** founder seniority and references (TO VALIDATE against the founder's CV).

## 3. Vytalix Consulting — ten fixed-price packages

### C1 — Digital Health Discovery Sprint
- **Scope:** "We know we need digital but not what, for whom, or what it costs." Problem framing, 10–15 user and stakeholder interviews, option appraisal, a 90-day roadmap. No build.
- **Deliverables:** problem statement; interview synthesis; three-option appraisal with cost ranges; 90-day roadmap; a 45-minute readout.
- **Duration:** 2 weeks.
- **Indicative price:** £14,000 (ESTIMATE); 8 founder days + 2 associate days.
- **Who it is for:** private providers, NHS service lines, health-tech vendors (S1, S3).
- **Outcomes:** a decision on what to build, buy or stop, with a plan the organisation can fund.

### C2 — Business Case & Funding Sprint
- **Scope:** a board-ready or HM Treasury Green Book-style five-case business case (strategic, economic, commercial, financial, management) with a benefits model and funding routes.
- **Deliverables:** business case document; benefits and cost model (spreadsheet); funding-route options; one revision round after board feedback.
- **Duration:** 3 weeks.
- **Indicative price:** £18,000 (ESTIMATE); 10 founder days.
- **Who it is for:** NHS programme leads, charities, NGOs (S3, S4).
- **Outcomes:** a case that can be submitted for internal approval, a grant or a donor.

### C3 — Digital Maternity Readiness Review
- **Scope:** for maternity units adopting digital records or responding to CNST Maternity Incentive Scheme data actions and CQC findings: current-state map, gap analysis against the Three Year Delivery Plan and MSDS requirements, prioritised plan. Not a clinical audit.
- **Deliverables:** current-state map; gap analysis; prioritised 12-month plan; leadership workshop.
- **Duration:** 4 weeks.
- **Indicative price:** £24,000 (ESTIMATE); 12 founder days.
- **Who it is for:** Heads of Midwifery, Maternity Digital Leads, Divisional Directors, private maternity units (S3).
- **Outcomes:** a defensible plan mapped to the standards the unit is already measured against.
- **Proof required to sell:** a clinical adviser (midwife or obstetrician) on the team — TO RECRUIT; not sold before then.

### C4 — UK Market-Entry Playbook for Health-Tech
- **Scope:** for non-UK health-tech companies wanting NHS customers: NHS structure brief, buyer map, assurance checklist (DTAC, DCB0129, DSPT, MHRA), pricing benchmarks, 12-month entry plan.
- **Deliverables:** playbook document; buyer map; assurance checklist; pricing benchmark note; 12-month plan; two working sessions.
- **Duration:** 4 weeks.
- **Indicative price:** £22,000 (ESTIMATE); 10 founder days.
- **Who it is for:** US, EU and African health-tech scale-ups (S1).
- **Outcomes:** the company knows who buys, what evidence they require and what it will cost to be ready.
- **Proof required to sell:** case examples and an NHS-experienced associate (TO ENGAGE on signed work).

### C5 — Africa Market-Entry & Partner Scan
- **Scope:** for UK/EU vendors and NGOs entering Nigeria, Kenya or Ghana: country scorecard, regulatory brief (NDPA 2023, NAFDAC where relevant, NHIA/SHA), partner long-list, entry-model options.
- **Deliverables:** scorecard; regulatory brief; partner long-list (20+ organisations, public information only); entry-model options paper; readout.
- **Duration:** 4 weeks.
- **Indicative price:** £20,000 (ESTIMATE); 10 founder days.
- **Who it is for:** health-tech companies, NGOs, foundations (S1, S4).
- **Outcomes:** a ranked view of where and how to enter, and whom to talk to first.
- **Proof required to sell:** in-country associate network (TO BUILD); until then, scope is desk-based and priced accordingly.

### C6 — DTAC & Evidence Readiness Sprint
- **Scope:** for vendors blocked by NHS assurance: DTAC self-assessment, clinical safety case skeleton (DCB0129), DSPT plan, NICE Evidence Standards Framework tier mapping. Not a substitute for the vendor's own Clinical Safety Officer sign-off.
- **Deliverables:** DTAC self-assessment with gap list; safety case skeleton and initial hazard log; DSPT action plan; evidence-tier mapping; 12-week readiness sequence.
- **Duration:** 3 weeks.
- **Indicative price:** £15,000 (ESTIMATE); 8 founder days.
- **Who it is for:** health-tech vendors (S1).
- **Outcomes:** the vendor can answer a buyer's DTAC questions with documents rather than promises.
- **Proof required to sell:** a contracted Clinical Safety Officer for the safety-case element (TO CONTRACT).

### C7 — Grant & Investor Readiness Sprint
- **Scope:** for founders who need a fundable narrative and a grant pipeline: investor deck review and rewrite, financial-model sanity check, grant calendar (Innovate UK, NIHR, SBRI Healthcare and relevant foundations), three bid outlines.
- **Deliverables:** revised deck; model review note; grant calendar; three bid outlines; a mock pitch session.
- **Duration:** 2 weeks.
- **Indicative price:** £9,500 (ESTIMATE); 6 founder days.
- **Who it is for:** pre-seed and seed health-tech founders (S1).
- **Outcomes:** a deck and a bid plan the founder can use the following week; no funding outcome is promised.

### C8 — Health Inequalities & Maternity Outcomes Review
- **Scope:** for trusts, ICBs and charities responding to MBRRACE-UK disparities, CQC or Ockenden-style findings: data review, a listening exercise with service users via community partners, an equity action plan and a measurement framework. Uses aggregate data only; no patient-level data is processed by Vytalix.
- **Deliverables:** data review; listening-exercise report; equity action plan; measurement framework; board presentation.
- **Duration:** 5 weeks.
- **Indicative price:** £28,000 (ESTIMATE); 14 founder days.
- **Who it is for:** trusts, ICBs, maternity charities (S3).
- **Outcomes:** an action plan grounded in what women and staff actually said, with measures the board can track.
- **Proof required to sell:** community-partner relationships (none claimed today; to be built with paid briefs).

### C9 — Programme Evaluation Lite
- **Scope:** for donors, NGOs and state programmes needing an evaluation of a digital-health programme: theory of change, indicators, mixed-methods review, report. Professional interviews and programme data only; not a clinical study.
- **Deliverables:** theory of change; indicator framework; evaluation report with limitations; recommendations; donor-ready summary.
- **Duration:** 6 weeks.
- **Indicative price:** £26,000 (ESTIMATE); ~12 founder days plus associate days.
- **Who it is for:** NGOs, foundations, state programmes (S4).
- **Outcomes:** an honest account of what the programme did and what to change; material for the next funding cycle.
- **Proof required to sell:** partnership with a university group for rigour where the client requires it (TO ESTABLISH).

### C10 — Corporate Workshop (one day)
- **Scope:** a facilitated one-day workshop for 15–25 people on a defined topic: digital maternity essentials, selling to the NHS, intended use and medical-device boundaries, safe use of AI in health operations, grant readiness.
- **Deliverables:** pre-workshop survey; materials; the session (on-site or virtual); a follow-up note with actions.
- **Duration:** 1 day (plus preparation).
- **Indicative price:** £4,000 (ESTIMATE; range £2,500–£6,000); 1.5 founder days. Cohort programmes £15k–£40k.
- **Who it is for:** NHS maternity and digital teams, private providers, NGOs, health-tech scale-ups (S3, S4, S5).
- **Outcomes:** a shared vocabulary and an action list; no accreditation or CPD points are claimed.

## 5. What we do not sell

- Clinical services, clinical audits or anything requiring a registered clinician's judgement about an individual patient.
- MaternaLink (in development) as a finished product. It is available only as a pilot or design-partnership conversation with explicit gates (`/assets/PARTNERSHIP_DECK.md`; pricing ranges in `/assets/PRICING_STRUCTURE.md` §5).
- Regulatory or legal advice presented as certainty; we map the pathway and name when to instruct qualified advisers.
- Work that requires Vytalix to process patient data under an advisory or consulting contract.

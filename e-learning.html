Accreditation status

Accreditation is being explored. Our courses are not yet accredited. Vytalix holds no CPD accreditation and no endorsement from the Royal College of Midwives, the Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists, the Nursing and Midwifery Council, any university or any professional council in Nigeria, Kenya or Ghana. Courses are designed to support continuing professional development and issue a certificate of completion only. We will not imply CPD points anywhere until an accreditation is actually granted.

Themes

Five themes, all of them things we do rather than things we read about

01

Digital maternity

Digital records, the maternity data standard, communication tools, and what changes on a ward and in a community team when the system goes live.

02

Care coordination in low-resource settings

Two-way SMS, offline-first tools, defaulter tracing, community health worker workflows, and programme reporting that funders can trust.

03

Health-tech commercialisation in the NHS

How the NHS buys, the assurance stack (DTAC, DCB0129, DSPT, Cyber Essentials), the medical-device line, and pricing that survives procurement.

04

Grants and investor readiness

Reading a call, structuring a bid, building a financial model an angel can interrogate, and the claims you are allowed to make at your stage.

05

Data protection and safe AI in care

UK GDPR for health teams in plain language, DPIA triggers, what "non-diagnostic" means in practice, and where AI helps without touching clinical decisions.

How we write clinical content

Every item with clinical content has a named clinical author and a named reviewer, a review date, cited sources (NICE, RCOG, WHO, national guidelines) and a note that it does not replace local clinical guidance. Courses that need a clinical co-author are not released until one is appointed.

Accreditation: what we are exploring

Three routes, none granted, none guaranteed:

  1. An independent CPD accreditation service for individual courses — typically weeks to months and a fee per course.
  2. A university partnership for credit-bearing modules.
  3. Professional-body endorsement processes, which are institution-specific and slow.

Until any of these is granted, every certificate we issue says "certificate of completion" and nothing more. Professional councils in Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana have their own CPD-point rules; we do not imply points there either.
